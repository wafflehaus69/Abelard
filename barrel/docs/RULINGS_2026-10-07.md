### Ruling 2, one consequence stated so it is not found later (builder's reading, not the ruling's words)

Actors are components (R1, and R2 for blocks). A delivered wallet is the creator's actor; if that wallet's own SOL funder is purpose-built, the funder and every other wallet it paid are therefore the creator's actor too. That is the builder's reading of "whoever paid its SOL" together with the standing collapse rule, it is what the code does, and a test pins it (`tests/test_actors_forms_parity.py`). A delivered wallet whose SOL came from an exchange, a hub or nobody joins the creator alone.

### Ruling 3, what the rows already held show before A1

The transaction rule can only exclude a transfer made in the slot of the member's first action. The rows of 2026-10-01 counted funding strictly before the second of the first action (`s.block_time < m.t_act`), so they never counted such a transfer; the rows of 2026-10-02 (the slot rule of MR-15) were the first to count it. With the exceptions listed below, a member the rule can move is therefore one whose funder already differs between those two results. `python barrel/recon/rent_rule_report.py --bound pbday`, graduations of 2026-09-01, 1,087 tokens, 76,999 members:

| Side of the set | Members | Net change of funder | Of which |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bundle groups not paid by the creator** (creation-slot traders, same-funder count of five or more) | 38, in 9 groups in 8 tokens | **0** | no funder count differs in any group; all 38 have a funder in both results |
| Creator and the wallets it paid | 76,961 | 342 net, in 332 tokens | 303 had no funder and gained one; 301 more stand behind the creator (295 on exactly the token-account rent, recorded in `B1A_GROUPED_VALIDATION.md`); 39 left a named sender (39 groups); 41 of the 342 ended behind a sender other than the creator |

What this establishes, and what it does not:

* **Bundle groups: no funder differs for any of the 38 members.** On that day the case the third review described (wallets funded and made to buy in one transaction by a common sender) did not occur among bundle groups the creator did not pay. Another 149 wallets at or above the bundle cut are on the creator's side (141 paid by the creator, 8 creators, in 28 tokens); they are inside the second row, and group rows cannot count them apart. A1's member form can: `compare_b1a_forms.py` reports the creator-paid ones (how many, and how many have no funder); the creators among them are not counted apart.
* **Creator's side: 342 is a net count per token, a floor and not a ceiling.** Group rows do not name members, so opposite moves inside one token cancel; the funder counts alone allow up to 1,546. It is exact on the 651 tokens whose side has one member (13 differ). 301 of the 342 are the creator's own payments; under ruling 2 those wallets are the creator's actor whether or not a funder is named, so the exclusion no longer costs them their resolution.
* **Outside this comparison altogether:**
  * 8 members in 5 tokens, all on the creator's side, first acquire on or after 2026-09-03, where both held results stop scanning for funders; the text on disk scans to 2026-09-12. At A1 their difference also carries the longer scan.
  * a member whose in-transaction sender was already its funder in the rows of 2026-10-01, when another wallet's last transfer outside that transaction is later than that sender's own. The other wallet can have paid in any slot of the second of the first action up to the action slot, or in the same earlier second as the sender, where the 2026-10-01 text chose between them arbitrarily. Both held results then name the in-transaction sender, and the rule moves the member to the other wallet.
* **A1 counts the same way**, net per token and side, against the rows of 2026-10-02, in groups and in members. That is exact for a bundle group with one funder. An exact count per member needs a column in the query (transfers excluded per group, by whether the sender is the creator). That would change a text that has not yet completed a run, so it is not made without an order.
* **It is one post-BOOST day, and bundle groups are thin on it.** The rows held for June 2025 carry more (96 bundle members in 6 of 236 tokens on 2025-06-09; 571 in 41 of 1,523 tokens over the week), but both June results predate the slot rule, so no difference can be taken there; the script refuses that pairing.
