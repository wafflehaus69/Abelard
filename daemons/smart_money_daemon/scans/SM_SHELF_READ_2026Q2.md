## 3. What this data will NOT support

- **13F is long-only and US-listed.** No shorts, no swaps, no non-US lines. A
  filer can appear long and be net short.
- **A put is not a short.** 13F does not disclose whether an option is bought or
  written, and option values are NOTIONAL, not premium or delta. Put notional is
  summed as positive ($77.671bn at Q2) — it is not capital and does not offset
  common one-for-one.
- **45-day lag.** Q2 marks are 2026-06-30, filed August, read later still.
- **corporate_strategic is not a view.** Alphabet/Amazon/NVIDIA are 21.5% of the
  book and mark balance-sheet stakes. Never pool them into a consensus.
- **Floored filers are partial in signal.** Horizon Kinetics contributes 37 of
  351 positions to signal, First Eagle 67 of 424. Their books are whole; their
  *votes* are the top slice.
- **SpaceX is a listing, not a purchase.** SPCX appears in exactly one period,
  first priced 2026-06-12. Seven filers show it NEW totalling **$122.32bn** at an
  identical $170.86 mark; the four large stakes are pre-existing private holdings
  becoming reportable. Any "biggest new position" table including it measures a
  listing. Shelf common Q2 = $762.41bn incl., **$640.09bn excl.**

## 4. Integrity caveats

**Three filers did not file Q2 and their last book must not read as current.**
Pershing Square (Ackman) filed 8 consecutive quarters through Q1 then stopped —
its $13.71bn / 11 positions (Brookfield $2.42bn, Amazon $2.39bn, Uber $2.16bn)
have **no Q2 observation**; held, sold or confidential is unknown from this
corpus. Scion (Burry) is absent three consecutive quarters. Founders Fund VII has
no Q1 or Q2.

**First Eagle's MSTR convertible (CUSIP 594972AS0) is off by 1000x.**
Q1: value 16,590,000,000 on PRN 20,000,000,000. Q2: 17,236,000 on PRN 20,000,000.
Both price to a plausible 83.0c / 86.2c per $1 principal, so one filing misstates
principal by 1000x. `value_scale` is 1 in both and cannot catch it — the error is
inside a single filing's own row, not the filing's unit. **This one row is 89% of
the shelf's Q1 convertible book**, and it means First Eagle did NOT shrink 21%:
its common book went $58.91bn → $59.84bn, roughly flat. Not verified against the
source filing; do not trust either leg.

**Two wrong-company price joins, both FIXED 2026-08-18** (`manual` pins):
- `067901108` BARRICK GOLD was mapped to **ABX**, which is *Abacus Global
  Management* (CIK 1814287) with 283 price rows — a wrong-NUMBER join on $2.96bn,
  not a missing one. Now pinned to **B** (Barrick Mining Corp, CIK 756894).
- `30063P105` EXACT SCIENCES was mapped to **EXK**, which is *Endeavour Silver*
  (CIK 1277866), 527 price rows. No verifiable replacement exists — the issuer is
  absent from the SEC ticker index and EXAS has no rows here — so it is now
  **unmapped by ruling**. Unmapped beats mapped-to-the-wrong-company.
Both had zero rows at Q2; the exposure was historical (2024-09 to 2025-03).

**4.3% of Q2 dollars have no ticker** (all CINS/foreign lines). Marks otherwise
validate: 1,086 of 1,098 Q2 common tickers priced, 938 within 5%, 0 outliers in
472 filer-cusip-period cells with ≥3 holders.

**96.4% of filings carry no cover-page control total**, so parse completeness is
unverified for all but Elliott.
